Annual General Body Meeting -31st Oct,2010


By admin; Published 25 Oct 2010

The Annual Council Elections of the Bangalore Astronomical Society is scheduled for the 31st of October 2010 at 4.00 pm at Cubbon park,(in-front of the Indrapriyadarshini Library).All members of the Society are invited to join us for the meeting and exercise their right to vote for a council, that will help BAS to grow to newer heights.

The Venue map link: http://wikimapia.org/#lat=12.975453&lon=77.5905374&z=19&l=0&m=b

If you wish to be a part of the council and help in the administration of the organisation then you are encouraged to contest for the elections as a candidate.
