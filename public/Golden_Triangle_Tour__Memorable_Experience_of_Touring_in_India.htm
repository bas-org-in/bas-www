Golden Triangle Tour- Memorable Experience of Touring in India


By michaelsm; Published 22 Oct 2009

India is one of the most
beautiful and charming tourist destinations in the world. If you love to
experience memorable tour experience in India, Golden Triangle Tour would be
the best. It is the most famous travel circuit that offers golden opportunities
for tourists to explore the cultural and heritage charm of there main cities of
Northern India- Delhi, Agra and Jaipur. These cities of India are well known
for their amazing tourism options, colourful fairs and festivals and heritage
monumental attraction that never fails to lure the heart of tourists. Every
year hundred and thousands of tourists from all over the world come here in
India to enjoy leisure and memorable holidays.

 



This amazing tour package takes
you way back to the medieval era and offers you to see and enjoy the ideal
blend of culture and history. From the brilliant architectural structure of the
Mughals to the royal palaces of the Rajput the tour offers amazing touring
options to enchant the tourists. Go and enjoy the Golden Triangle Tour and
explore the historical architectures of Delhi, royal and eye catching monuments
and palace of Jaipur and Taj Mahal and Agra Fort in Agra.  



 



Touring in the Delhi the capital
city of India, tourists can enjoy visiting to some of the splendor monuments which
depicts the love of the Mughal rulers towards art and culture. In this city
there are several eye catching tourist attractions which are worth visiting and
exploring that displays the glorious past. Red Fort (World Heritage Fort
Declared by UNESCO), Qutb Minar (Tallest Brick Minaret in the World), Jama
Masjid (Largest Mosque in India), India Gate, Lotus Temple, National Museum,
President House, etc are some of the worth visiting monuments and places of
tourist interest.



 



Agra
the second travel destination of the Golden
triangle tour package is proud of housing one of the Seven Wonders
of the World- Taj Mahal. This beautiful monument of India is situated on the
bank of River Yamuna is the symbol of epitome of beauty and symbol of
everlasting love. Taj Mahal the heritage monument of India was built by the
broken hearted ruler Saha Jahan in memory of his beloved Mumtaz Mahal. Awesome
beauty of Taj Mahal appeals tourists from all the corner of the world for tours
and travels in India. Other tourist attractions of the city are Agra Fort and
Fatehpur Sikri.  



 



Jaipur the capital city of Rajasthan
is the final travel destination of the tour circuit. It is globally famous for
its heritage and monumental attractions that display the glorious past and the
love of the rajas and maharajas towards art and culture. In this city there are
several eye catching monuments which are worth visiting and exploring. Amber
Fort, Nahargarh Fort, Jaigarh Fort, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal, Laxmi Narayan
Temple, etc are some the famous tourists attraction of Jaipur.



 



Beside Jaipur tourists can also
enjoy Rajasthan tours by visiting to some magnificent cities of
Rajasthan. Almost all the cities of Rajasthan are dotted with magnificent
monuments, palatial palaces, grand havellis, invincible forts, colourful fairs
and festivals and many other great tourism attraction of Rajasthan. Touring to
the bustling cities of Rajasthan will truly be the delightful experience of Rajasthan
India tours which
tourists will love to cherish for lifetime.
