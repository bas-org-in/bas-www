Minutes from "School Outreach at Lakshmisagara village." on 02/29/2008 - 15:00


By naveenln; Published 03 Mar 2008

It was fantastic outreach program for kannada medium village students of 8th and 9th standard.

I discussed the basic structure of solar system.  Solar system was illustrated in action with students participating as planets, sun and comet.

 The saturn rings and comet's tail was fantastic :)

1.5 hours just passed by with these kids and the questions from all over the class, I was satisfied. I have lit the candle in their mind.

 Later I had a naked eye observation session. stellar evolution and stars were discussed.
