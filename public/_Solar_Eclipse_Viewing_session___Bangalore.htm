Solar Eclipse Viewing session @ Bangalore


By vivek; Published 13 Jan 2010

The Bangalore Astronomical Society(BAS) in Collaboration with the Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA)  and BGVS is organising a Solar Eclipse Viewing session on the 15th of January 2010.  The details of the event are as under

DATE: 15th January 2010
VENUE: 18th Cross Grounds , Malleshwaram,Bangalore (opposite the 18th Cross Bus stand)
TIME: 11.00 am onwards



Please note:
* We intend to view and show case  the eclipse using Projection techniques.
* You may purchase Eclispe Viewing Goggles from the Planitarium.
* Please contact us for any further details on info@bas.org.in
