Public skywatch event at Hymanshu Jyothi Kala Peetha School


By p6; Published 08 Mar 2007

Public skywatch event to be held at Hymanshu Jyothi Kala Peetha school, Malleshwaram. The session is being organised for school students and shall involve observation of the half-moon, Orion Nebula, Saturn, M44, M41, M35.. etc. For further information, please contact Shashank H J
