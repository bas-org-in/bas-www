Yelagiri Star Party


By hemanthariyani; Published 31 Mar 2008

As the observing season ends, this might be our last star party for a few months to come. Dr. Suresh and friends from Chennai will be joining us at Yelagiri. Landmark hotel details are being worked out by Dr. Suresh and as of now the deal with the resort is that we will be charged Rs 1300/- PER PERSON including stay and food. This amount needs to be paid to the hotel directly. Transportation to yelairi and back will be worked out once we have enough information about participants.
