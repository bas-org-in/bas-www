A Night with the stars @ Parikaram school,Jayanagar


By vivek; Published 28 Jan 2010

The Bangalore Astronomical Society as part of its Outreach programme, will be organizing a sky watch session  for the students of the Parikrama school at their Jayanagar Premises. The event is scheduled for 7 pm and last for about 2 hours or so. The programme should involve a night sky viewing session , with a small elementary talk on astronomy and the solar system.The main telescope that will be used is the BAS 6" Dobsonian Telescope.

Event Coordinator: Vivek Yerkadithaya
Volunteers: Gautham G. Ambekar
