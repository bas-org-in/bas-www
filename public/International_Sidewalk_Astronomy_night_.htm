International Sidewalk Astronomy night.


By p6; Published 23 Apr 2007

19th of May is regarded as the International Sidewalk Astronomy event.
From the BAS perspective, we intend to do some sidewalk astronomy events at various places within the city in the wake of this ocassion.

This event is still being planned. If you'd like to lend a helping hand, Please contact us.

Further developments shall be updated in this space.
