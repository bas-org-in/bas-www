VENUES


By admin; Published 10 Sep 2009

Meetings -



Meetings are normally held at Cubbon Park, In front of Indira Priyadarshini Childrens’ Library.

1. From Vidhana Soudha -
Walk past the same and in front of Vikas Soudha ( Vidhana Soudha Jr ). Herein, come into Cubbon Park. Walk past KGS Club which is 200m in the vicinity of the front entrance. Indira Priyadarshini Library is less than 50m ahead.
2. From Kasturba Road -
Walk into Cubbon Park and all the way until Sheshadri Iyer public library ( Big red building ).. Walk around the same and Indira Priyadarshini Library is a little ahead unto the left.

Star Parties and Observations -



These are held at Hosahalli - aboiut 20KM off Doddaballapur, approximatelely 70KM from Bangalore in all.

Route Summary -

Bangalore -> Hebbala -> Yelahanka / Devanahalli -> Doddaballapur -> Green Valley Resort -> Hosahalli

1. By personal transport -
Get to Doddballapur ( through Yelahanka/Devanahalli ) and get to “D Cross” by asking for directions. Herein, follow the road through “TB Cross”. About 5KM ahead, At Ghati Cross where the road forks, Take the road on the left. About 5KM ahead, You will find a deviation to the left ( A board too, That says “4KM to Green Valley Resort” ).. Take this deviation and donot take further deviations anywhere. The road is rugged and cratered. Hosahalli is less than 12KM from here. For further Directions, Refer the Wikimapia link below.
2. By Bus -
You can board a bus to Doddballapur from Cauvery Bhavan / Hebbal / Yelahanka. At Doddaballapur, take a bus to Hosahalli. Please ask for and confirm that your destination is Hosahalli enroute Ujjini and Kottige manchenahalli. Get off at Hosahalli school/police station.
