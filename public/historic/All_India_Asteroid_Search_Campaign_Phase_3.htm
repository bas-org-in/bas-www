All India Asteroid Search Campaign Phase 3


By keerthi2kiran; Published 05 Jul 2012

Hi All,

Hope some of you enjoyed being part of the All India Asteroid Search Campaign Phase 2, organized by SPACE, Delhi,India (http://www.space-india.com) in Collaboration with International Astronomical Search Collaboration, Texas,USA (http://iasc.hsutx.edu).
We are happy to announce that, we have been given a slot to participate in Phase 3 as well.
To participate in Phase 3 of the Campaign, Please send me your Name, Email Address, Contact Number, & the Location. Based on these information we will form a team & you & your team members can meet & discuss & do Astrometry. Since there is a limit on number of image sets we receive, hence the preference will be given to first come, first serve basis, hurry up & register to participate & discover new Asteroids.
Those who participated in Phase 2 can also register for Phase 3. But first preference will be given to the new participants. After the newly registered members, based on the availability of the image set the Phase 2 participants will get to work on the image set if any unassigned image set is available.
Astrometry & other details will be sent to the registered participants.
Thanks & Regards,
Kiran Kumar Tikare
