"Astronomy at Schools" organised for Adarsha Vidyalaya, R.T.Nagar, Bangalore


By p6; Published 02 Dec 2008

As part of BAS Outreach events and popularisation of astronomy at schools, BAS has scheduled school outreach for Adarsha Vidyalaya on 6th Dec 2008 for their students.


This is a One full day event. Topics covered are,

Introduction to Astronomy, telescopes, night sky

Constellations

Our Solar system

Stellar evolution

Our Galaxy

Universe


Learning with observation and experiments. Interactive sessions, followed by observation session.


Duration : 1 day ( 8:30 am - 8:00 pm )


Event Co-ordinator : Naveen Nanjundappa
