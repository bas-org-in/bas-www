BAS Elects New Council


By p6; Published 25 Oct 2007

Here's the much awaited report! We broke it on the mailing lists earlier though.
Here is the new BAS Council

Hemant Hariyani retains the office of the President.
Naveen L N retains the office of the Vice President.
Pavan retains the office of the Secretary.
Chandrashekar R is the new Joint Secretary!
Naveen Chandra is the new Treasurer!
Amar Sharma to take care of the Observation front of things, as ever.
Akarsh Simha to take care of the Scientific, Projects front of things.
Shweta Sharma has offered to help with her time on the ATM, instrumentation domain!
Naveen L N is also going to handle the Outreach Dept along with the office of the Vice President
Rakesh, Harshad - Formerly the Tresurer and the Joint Secy continue to hold council positions.
Venkatesh Soundararajan is another new face in council!

It was an eventful first year. We'll look forward to making this one just as eventful if not more. We seek your willful cooperation and wishes on this endeavour to spread awareness about Astronomy as a passion, hobby and science.
