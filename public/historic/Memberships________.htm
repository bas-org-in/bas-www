In order to apply for Membership to BANGALORE ASTRONOMICAL SOCIETY , kindly follow the steps mentioned below:



STEP 1:

Download  the application form.
APPLICATION FORM

STEP 2:

Fill the Application Form in BLOCK LETTERS and attach your recent Passport size photograph on the space provided.

STEP 3:

Pay the membership fee in one of the following two ways:

You may transfer the membership fee via Online transfer to our ICICI Bank Account.
Our ICICI Bank A/C No. 004701046124

                                                                                OR

You may make a Demand draft/cheque in favour of BANGALORE ASTRONOMICAL SOCIETY for the prescribed membership fee.

STEP 4:

Send the following documents via Post or through a recognised Courier service.
> Filled Application Form.
> Cheque / DD / Details of your On-line Transfer
> one extra Stamp size photograph
> One Self addressed stamped envelope
> An attested photocopy of your Photo ID proof (Eg: Pan Card,Driving Liscence,Passport etc. )
> A Photocopy of Valid Student ID card (for student memberships only)

Send the above mentioned documents to the following address
