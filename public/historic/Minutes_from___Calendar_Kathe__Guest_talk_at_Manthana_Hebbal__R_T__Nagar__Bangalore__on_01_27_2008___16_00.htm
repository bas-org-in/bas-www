Minutes from ""Calendar Kathe" Guest talk at Manthana Hebbal, R.T. Nagar. Bangalore" on 01/27/2008 - 16:00


By naveenln; Published 28 Jan 2008

"Calendar kathe" was successfully delivered.

Basically the talk pointed out the

1. Need for calendar

2. Use of calendar

3. Elements of Calendar

4. Types of calendar ( lunar, solar and lunar-solar )

5. calendar as used by Hindu, Islamic and western countries.

6. Time measurements.

7. corrections appiled and current day calendar.

 There were over 30+ people, overall the discussion went on for 1.5 hours and we everyone appreciated the discussion and topic.

It was a different experience for me too, this being the first talk in kannada.
