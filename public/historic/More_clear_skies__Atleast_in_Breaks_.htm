More clear skies, Atleast in Breaks!


By p6; Published 06 Sep 2007

Just a couple of days back or so, Ravindra was at my place too. We happened to look up South, and Saggitarius shone amazingly bright!

The M22 milky way patch was clearly conspicuous with some looking, and from under a tubelight too. The sky was super transparent. M7 was naked eye, rather easily.

In the north, The summer triangle of Altaire, Vega and Deneb was high up and Andromeda, Pegasus Square rose up just a little lower down.

Naveen too, reported later on that M31 was naked eye!

I hope to god it stays good when we go out observing next, There's nothing like the bright evening southerly milkyway this time of the year !
