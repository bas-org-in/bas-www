BAS Election Day 2007


By p6; Published 24 Sep 2007

It's that time of the year again! The last and the first BAS election happened on the 16th of October last year and now it's the end of a 1 year term for us.

This 14th Oct, being the sunday closest to the 16th has been chosen for the Election Day. The agenda for the day shall be to renominate the administrative council and subsequently elect the office bearers. This agenda shall be preceeded by an yearly report and recap of important events that have happened.

We invite potential contenders to the council and subsequently the offices, to be present on time during the evening. Contact us for any queries. Go here for venue information.
