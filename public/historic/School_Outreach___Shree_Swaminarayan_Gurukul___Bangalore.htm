School Outreach @ Shree Swaminarayan Gurukul - Bangalore


By naveenln; Published 10 Apr 2008

An outreach event has been planned for High school students at Shree Swaminarayan Gurukul - Bangalore

 Agenda of the outreach are

1. Basic observation tips

2. Mars, Moon, open clusters observation.

3. Discussion to create interest in Astronomy.

4. Astronomy film show ( if possible ) 

Instruments : 7X50 Binocular and 6 inch reflector telescope.

you are welcome to join this session :)
