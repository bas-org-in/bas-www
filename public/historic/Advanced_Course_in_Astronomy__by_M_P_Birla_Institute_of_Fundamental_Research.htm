Advanced Course in Astronomy by M.P.Birla Institute of Fundamental Research


By stephen_hans2002; Published 22 Feb 2008

Dear All,

We, at the M.P.Birla Institute of Fundamental Research, Bangalore are
planning to conduct advance courses in the following topics.

1. Solar Astronomy ............(by Dr.K.B.Ramesh)
2. Radio Astronomy ...........(by Prof. K.R.Subramanian)
3. Astrobiology ..................(by Prof. C.Sivaram)
4. Astrochemistry ..............(by Prof. C.Sivaram)

Each of these courses will be covered in 18 sessions (duration of each
session is 90 minutes).

The classes will be conducted as week-end classes and evening classes
during the months of February and March 2008.

The syllabus of each course will be at M.Sc. level.Even B.Sc Students can also apply

Certificates with grades will be awarded after the successful
completion of each course.

Interested students may please contact us through email as soon as
possible.
