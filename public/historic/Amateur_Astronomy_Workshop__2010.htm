Amateur Astronomy Workshop -2010


By admin; Published 25 Oct 2010

 

Date:24th &25th July,2010

Venue:National College,Jayanagar,Bangalore

Time: 9.00 am to 5.00 pm on both days 

Amateur Astronomy is a fascinating field and many of us have at some point in our lives had a fascination to learn about Outer-Space and The night sky . This enthusiasm and interest is very easily lost and is buried .People generally tend to lose interest because they don’t get the correct answers and solutions to questions like  “WHAT DO I DO LEARN MORE ?”  ”WHICH TELESCOPE SHOULD I USE?” “HOW DO I EXPLORE THE SKY AND SEE THE CELESTIAL OBJECTS?”. Well, The Bangalore Astronomical Society believes that these concerns needs proper  redressal . To answer a beginners concerns ,channelize Astronomy buffs and enthusiasts and to experience the night sky in a whole new way, BAS is organizing an  AMATEUR ASTRONOMY WORKSHOP on the 24th and 25th of July 2010. The workshop will be conducted by very experienced Amateur Astronomers ,Astro-Photography experts and Instrumentation specialists from the Organization. It will serve as an ideal platform for those who wish to Learn the art of navigating in the sky. The Workshop is open to all above the age of 15.  Registrations for the Workshop have begun and they end on the 22nd of July 2010. The mode and details of Registration are as under:

Who should attend this workshop?

Practically anyone can attend this. Not necessary you have astronomy as your subject. Even folks with commerce, arts or varying backgrounds are encouraged to share the spirit of amateur astronomy. But we are encouraging students and young alike to participate, since it can help hone their budding skills and passion towards the night sky, and be sparked if they were possibly even thinking of a career in astronomy. 

What are the prerequisites for the workshop?

Ever wondered what amateur astronomy is all about? Ever had a hard time understanding some of the basic concepts? Ever felt what it feels like looking at the night sky? Paranoid of the jargon involved that advanced amateurs use? Pre-requisites to attend are just have the seething passion towards the night sky and astronomy.

What will I learn from the workshop?

The first day is aimed at elaborating on most of the concepts of the subject, starting from basic terms on Solar System, Galaxies and telescopes. Through multiple and group interaction, all queries one has should be addressed.

 What is the Age Group being addressed in the workshop

Age group targeted for making best use of workshop is anywhere from high school (9th or 10th std) until someone who is working. Basically, one who considers himself _fit_ for astronomy is invited to join, and confident to grasp what an amateur is laying in his words for another amateur.
