School Outreach Program @ Adarsha School, R.T. Nagar. Bangalore.


By naveenln; Published 10 Jan 2008

An outreach event has been planned for about 30-40 astronomy interested students at Adarsha School, R.T. Nagar. Bangalore.

 Agenda of the outreach are

1. Basic observation tips ( directions, measurements of angles, constellations)

2. Mars, Moon, open clusters observation.

3. Short talk on  Stellar evolution.

Venue : Adarsha School, R.T. Nagar. Bangalore.

 Time : 7:00 pm  till 10:00 pm

Instruments : 7X50 Binocular and 6 inch reflector telescope.
