BAS Annual Council Elections -2010-2011


By admin; Published 25 Oct 2010


 The Annual Council Elections of the Bangalore Astronomical Society is scheduled for the 31st of October 2010 at 4.00 pm at Cubbon park,(in-front of the Indrapriyadarshini Library).All members of the Society are invited to join us for the meeting and exercise their right to choose a council, that will help BAS to grow to newer heights.

If you wish to be a part of the council and help in the administration of the organisation then you are encouraged to contest for the elections as a candidate.
