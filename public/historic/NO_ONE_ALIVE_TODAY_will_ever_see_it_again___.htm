Well this mail has been floating around the world since 2003!

Let’s put in some thoughts on this mail. I am sure with the details below you will realise the fact about this mail.

The mean distance of Earth from Sun is 1.0 AU. And that of Mars from Sun is about 1.5 AU. The distance of Mars from Earth varies from 0.5 AU (closest 1.5 AU - 1.0 AU) and 2.5 AU (farthest 1.5 AU + 1.0 AU). Therefore, Mars changes its apparent size by a factor or ratio of about 2.5 AU/0.5 AU or 5:1!

Mars appeared only about 1/90th apparent size of the Full Moon (during 2005 November opposition). And about 1/75th (during 2003 August opposition.) That means, if you used a telescope with a magnification of about 90x and 75x respectively, the image of Mars in the telescope would appear about the same size as the Full Moon appears without a telescope.

Its simple, Mars is 6800 Kms across and our Moon 3476 kms across. Moon is about 4 lakh kms from Earth, that Mars twice the size of moon should be at most 8 lakh kms from earth for it to appear as big as full moon to naked eye. One can easily compare the distance, where is 8 Lakh Kms and 34.56 Million miles. !!!

With the simple calculation you will find out how smaller mars will be at that distance compared to moon. If mars happen to come as close as 8 lakh Kms to earth. the tides will rise high due to the gravitational force from moon and mars..

Mars is a beautiful red planet to see thro a telescope, never miss an opportunity if you have one.
