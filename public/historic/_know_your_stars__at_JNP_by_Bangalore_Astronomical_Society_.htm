"know your stars" at JNP by Bangalore Astronomical Society.


By naveenln; Published 13 Apr 2009

Please Join for

Bangalore Astronomical Society's Outreach program in collaboration with JN Planetarium.

Date : 09-May-2009
Time : 6:00 Pm

Details :
1. An introduction to night sky, Identification of Constellations and planetary locations.
2. Ask and Amateur Astronomer, where you can ask all your questions and discuss.
3. Observation of planets and moons ( Provided sky is clear )

Place :
Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium, Near Basaveshwara Circle, opp Chalukya hotel ( High grounds )

Event Co-ordinator : Naveen Nanjundappa.
