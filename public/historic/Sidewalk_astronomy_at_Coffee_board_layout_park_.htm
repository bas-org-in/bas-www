Sidewalk astronomy at Coffee board layout park,


By naveenln; Published 15 Feb 2012

Organised a 2 hour sidewalk astronomy session at the coffee board layout park, Hebbal, Bangalore.

About 150 people saw Planet Jupiter and Venus.
Celestron 8SE Computerized telescope was used for the event.
Co-ordinator: Naveen Nanjundappa.
Date: 15.02.2012
