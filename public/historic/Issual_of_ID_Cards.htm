Issual of ID Cards


By p6; Published 21 Sep 2007

BAS Registered Members who have not yet collected their ID Cards are hereforth requested to do so as early as they can. ID cards for approoved memberships shall hereinafter be issued on every meeting day.

The requisites for obtaining yours shall be to produce one stamp size photograph if not tendered already, and a valid proof of identity that could include your driving license, PAN Card, Ration Card or College/Work Identity cards. Please note that no ID cards shall be issued lest these be presented.

The next scheduled meeting is on the 23rd of September. You can find more information on this event posting in here and here. For further enquiries on ID card issual, Please contact Mr Naveen L N.
