Kalpaneya Yatre


By akarshsimha; Published 26 Nov 2010

Kalpaneya Yatre (Kannada for "Journey of Imagination") is an astronomy festival that is a joint effort of several organizations including ISRO, IIA and Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium, Bangalore. BAS is amongst the volunteers, and is involved in conducting the evening sky-watching events amongst other things.

Please read the event website to know more.
