The weather ?


By p6; Published 06 Jul 2007

Yeah..it's the thick of the rainy season, but IMHO..the weather has not been as good as last year.

We haven't had the rains, neither have we had the clear skies.. I can distinctly remember amar urging us to goto Hosahalli sometime in July last year...and there always seemed to be plenty of blue windows by the day time nevertheless..and it rained when it really had to..Unlike the cloudy, windy status quo B'lore seems to be experiencing off late.

Seems like we wont really be getting lucky on star parties in this season... Like the lucky break we got on Aug 24th last year...Any takers ?
