Side-Walk AStronomy@ BTM Layout,Bangalore (31st October 2009)


By vivek; Published 26 Oct 2009

The  Bangalore Astronomical Society as part of its "30 day sidewalk Astronomy" Program  will be organising a public sidewalk event at BTM layout on the 31st of October 2009 ,between 6.30 pm and 8.30 pm.
If the weather permits , we intend to show the Planet Jupiter and The Moon to the public through Astronomical Telescopes.

We invite you to join us on this day in viewing the marvels of our universe , the way Galileo did 400 years ago.
This is the last day of the  program ,so don't miss this opportunity !!
Please contact BAS-coordinator , Amar Sharma, for more details on the event
