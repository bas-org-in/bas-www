teach astronomy on summer camp for children


By Rachita; Published 15 Jan 2008

We are looking for someone who can teach astronomy to the children on the Youreka camp. Youreka is an exciting outdoor learning program, specially designed for 9 to 15 year olds. Children develop new skills, build on character traits and develop a confident personality. The program instills confidence through engaging activities, rich relationships and numerous ‘I did it' moments. Job opportunities We are expanding our pool of resource people. The people who work with us bring specific skills and capabilities that help in enriching the Youreka program. They conduct activities such as Rock Climbing, back packing, out door survival, astronomy, theatre, music and other such activities. We expect them be skilled and have experience in their field of interest to share with the children on the program. In addition to the activities, the resource person also helps in campus management, ensuring a safe and memorable experience. What’s in it for you? *An extremely rewarding experience (and not just monetarily)*Your work place is in the midst of breath taking surroundings- spectacular mountains, enchanting forests, beautiful rivers  *Your clientele will comprise of spirited and enthusiastic children, who might end up making you their role model J  *Your colleagues will be people from diverse backgrounds-expand your circle of friends*Lots of fun and a whole lot of learning!*An unforgettable experience which you will forever cherish                                                …………………….Still thinking??????   If you are interested in working on the Youreka programs or know someone who would be interested, please let me know!      Thank you!Looking forward to hearing from you rachita.singh@youreka.in
