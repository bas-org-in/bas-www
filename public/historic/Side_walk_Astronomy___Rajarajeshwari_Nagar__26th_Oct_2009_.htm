Side-walk Astronomy @ Rajarajeshwari Nagar (26th Oct 2009)


By vivek; Published 25 Oct 2009

As part of the "30 day Side walk Astronomy  programme" , the Bangalore Astronomical Society is conducting a sky watch session at Rajarajeshwari nagar ,Bangalore on the 26th of October 2009 between 6.30 pm and 8.30 pm.The event is free and open to all .We invite you to join us on the 26th to observe the marvels of the univers the way Galileo did 400 years ago.
Please contact Aparna from BAS for more details regarding the event.
