Presentation on Deep Sky Observing @ ABAA, JNP


By amar_universe; Published 18 May 2011

Title : Deep-Sky Observing

Speaker : Amar A. Sharma

Venue : Lecture Hall, Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium

Hosted by : Association of Bangalore Amateur Astronomers (ABAA)

Time : 17:30 - 18:30 hrs

Date : 22nd May 2011, Sunday

This presentation should serve as a backdrop for those keen enthusiasts who have always wondered what the sighting the wonders in the night sky viz. galaxies, clusters and nebulae is like, so you are prepared for the next observing session with a telescope.

It will cover some of the basic and advanced aspects needed to kick-start the art of DSO. Do be there and enrich the experience.

Note : It is a private event conducted by ABAA. So please do *not* contact the Planetarium for inquiries. Please contact me for any further queries on 9535098126.
