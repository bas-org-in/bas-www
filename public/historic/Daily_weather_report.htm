Daily weather report


By anand; Published 08 Mar 2009

You can check the daily weather and cloud movements over India on http://www.mosdac.gov.in/ here you can check daily satellite images on the right side of the webpage.To get the hour by hour updates click KALPANA1 Images on the left hand side of the webpage then it will ask for User ID, to access simply type your name and organization name in visitors area.
Another link where you can check the weather http://www.imd.gov.in/section/satmet/dynamic/insat.htm
