Astronomy Digital Picture of the Day


By naveenln; Published 21 Jan 2009

Astronomy Digital Picture of the Day (ADPD) blog is created to display astrophotographs captured using digital camera.

Visit : http://naveenln.wordpress.com/

Purpose of this blog is to
1. Encourage people use normal digital camera / DSLR / SLR for astrophotography.
2. Capture special moments of night sky.
3. Share Astronomy and astrophotography information.

All you need is a DIGITAL CAMERA and a TRIPOD.
