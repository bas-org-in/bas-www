Night Sky Observation Session at Government Science College, Bangalore


By naveenln; Published 24 Apr 2012

the Night Sky Observation Session at Government Science College is conducted by one of BAS volunteer Kiran Marlapudi on 27th April,. in association with the Department of Physics, Government Science College.


Date: 27.04.2012
Day: Friday
Time: 05:30pm to 8:00pm
Venue: Government Science College, Bangalore 560001
Telescope: 6inch Celestron Telescope.
agenda: There will be short talk by Kiran M on observational astronomy and about the different kinds of telescope followed by observation of Jupiter, Venus, Mars, Moon, Orion Nebula etc.. through telescope.
Volunteer: Kiran Marlapudi
