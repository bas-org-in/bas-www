* * *

Our knowledge of the universe has evolved over the millennia with the steady
advance in observational techniques and with the progress in theoretical
explanations and models. The culture of any civilization is an amalgam of
its myths and legends, art and literature, science and philosophy, religious
beliefs and superstitions. Cosmic vision has had its influence, often a
profound one, on culture in all its variety. This will be illustrated in the
presentation of Prof. Vishveshwara through astronomical photographs,
paintings, and quotations as well as short video clips of computer graphics
with background music.

* * *



*Prof. Vishveshwara* obtained his PhD from University of Maryland, College
Park, USA. He was a Senior Professor at Indian Institute of Astrophysics,
Founder and Honorary Director of Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium, Bangalore.
Vice-Chairman of Bangalore Association for Science Education, Fellow of
Indian Academy of Sciences, Member of International Astronomical Union
American Physical Society, International Society for General Relativity and
Gravitation. His interests are General theory of relativity, relativistic
astrophysics and cosmology. He has made major contributions to the theory of
Black hole. He has a number of technical and popular articles and Co-editor
of 11 books. He is author of the book "Einstein's Enigma or Black Holes in
My Bubble Bath", Published by Copernicus an imprint of Springer Verlag
(2006). He was a Visiting Professor of University of London, University of
Padua, Italy, University of Pennsylvania, University of Maryland,
Universidad del Pais Vasco, Bilbao, Spain.
