Star Party @ Om Shanti Dhama


By p6; Published 21 Nov 2007

BAS, in coordination with the Om Shanti Dhama trust is arranging a star party at the premises of Om Shanti Dhama gurukula, off Kanakapura, Bangalore

The agenda shall be primarily sky assessment, Deep sky Observation and a brief outreach event dedicated to the kids of the school.

Om Shanti Dhama is located at about 30KM distance from kanakapura, about 2KM off Mekedat Sangama. In all, about 100km from Bangalore, on the banks of Kaveri.
