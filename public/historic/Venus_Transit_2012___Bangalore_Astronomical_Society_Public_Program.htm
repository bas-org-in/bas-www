Venus Transit 2012 - Bangalore Astronomical Society Public Program


By naveenln; Published 04 Jun 2012

BAS in association with Karnataka Gyan Vigyan Samithi is pleased to announce the public outreach and observation event at Lalbagh. This is in association with Karnataka Gyan Vigyan Samithi.

Date : 6th June, 2012
Time : 6:30 am - 10:30 am
Place : Lalbagh ( small hill area )
Breakfast distribution by KGVS & BAS for all observers
General talks on Transit and discussion
Observation method :
Projection through Telescope,
Visual filter on binoculars,
Visual filter on telescopes,
Solar Goggles
Photography using filter though telescopes
Volunteers: Keerthi Kiran, Naveen, Achyut, Pramod, Basavaraj + KGVS volunteers and M.R.Nagaraju (popularly known as walking encyclopedia)
