Lunar Astrophotography @ BAS Monthly Projects - Feb 2009.


By naveenln; Published 28 Jan 2009

Bangalore Astronomical Society, as part of its outreach activities has planned for a monthy projects for IYA2009.


Project for Feb 2009. Lunar Astrophotography and Lunar Calendar


BAS welcome its members to submit their photos of moon taken between Feb 01, 2009 and Feb 26, 2009.


Agenda : Create BAS's Lunar Calendar with photos.


Take photo of moon every day, by 26.02.2009 so that BAS can get all the phase of moon.

One can use any camera with Tripod, to capture moon's phase.


Send in your daily photos ( best one ) to naveen.nanjundappa@gmail.com
