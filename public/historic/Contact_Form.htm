Contact Form


By akarshsimha; Published 22 Mar 2013

Use this form to contact the council of BAS.

Note: BAS is a non-profit organization run by volunteers. Most of the times there is insufficient manpower to reply to all e-mail that we receive. So, before you send out a mail...

  • check our knowledgebase to see if your question is answered there.

  • if your question is about astronomy, or telescopes, it is more likely to get a reply if asked on our Google group.

  • if you want notifications about future BAS events, you can join the Announcement Google group.



If your question is still unanswered, please contact us using the form below.
