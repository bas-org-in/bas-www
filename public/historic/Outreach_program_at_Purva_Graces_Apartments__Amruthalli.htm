Outreach program at Purva Graces Apartments, Amruthalli


By admin; Published 24 Oct 2010

An outreach program has been arranged for the Purva Graces Apartments residents, for popularizing astronomy, planetary observation and discussion with students of 8-12 years age group.

Venue: 

Purva Graces Apartments, Amruthalli, Off Bellary Road, Bangalore - 560092


Date/Day : 26th March 2010. Friday

Time : 7:00 pm - 9:00 pm


Agenda: IYA 2009 video show and planetary observation.


Co-ordinator: Naveen Nanjundappa ( 9980505003 )
