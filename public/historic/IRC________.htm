IRC


By akarshsimha; Published 22 Mar 2013

Please choose a relevant nick name, fill in the CAPTCHA, and click "Connect" to join our IRC (Internet Relay Chat) chatroom.

Note that our IRC chatroom is quite inactive, so you may have to join and leave your connection open for quite a while to get a reply. If you find the nicknames kstar or confoocious, try to type their nicknames in the chatroom to draw their attention.

Also, have you checked our knowledgebase?

If you wish to be a regular participant in the chat room, you may wish to learn to use an IRC client. Search the internet for a basic IRC tutorial. Other channels of interest include the more active ##astronomy channel on freenode.
