Pretty Interesting Coincidence


By p6; Published 18 Aug 2007

We all know July 22nd is now officially BAS's birthday..
Now then, July 22nd 2009 also happens to be the century's longest  Total solar eclipse with the totality spanning some 3 and a half minutes!

The eclipse passes through the heart of India, Starts off early in the morning at the Gujrat coast, Through MP et al through the morning and all the way further east..

I know the weather will be unpredictable, but we'd better make some really big plans for this event...and find out ways of getting lucky.
