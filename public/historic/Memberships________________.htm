MEMBERSHIP PACKAGES

 

  Regular Annual Membership
  • Annual Membership fee of               Rs. 200 / year
         Family Annual Membership
  • Annual Membership fee of Rs.400/Year for family of 4 .(An Additional of Rs.100 per person for families of over 4 people. )
  
   Student Annual Membership
  • Annual Membership fee of
         Rs. 100 / year.
  

    

In order to apply for Membership to The BANGALORE ASTRONOMICAL SOCIETY , kindly follow the steps mentioned below:

 

STEP 1:

Download  and Print the application form.
 APPLICATION FORM (Click here)

STEP 2:

Fill the Application Form in BLOCK LETTERS and paste your  photograph in the space provided.

STEP 3:

Pay the membership fee in one of the following two ways:

You may transfer the membership fee via Online transfer to our ICICI Bank Account.
Our ICICI Bank A/C No. 004701046124

                                                                                OR

You may make a Demand draft/cheque in favour of BANGALORE ASTRONOMICAL SOCIETY for the prescribed membership fee.

STEP 4:

Send the following documents via Post or through a recognised Courier service.

  1. Completed Application Form.
  2. Cheque / DD / Details of your On-line Transfer
  3. One  Stamp-size photograph
  4. One Self addressed  envelope with stamp.
  5. An attested photocopy of your Photo ID proof (Eg: Pan Card,Driving Liscence,Passport etc. )
  6. A Photocopy of Valid Student ID card (for student memberships only)


Send the above mentioned documents to the following address


Naveen Nanjundappa,

House no. 3, First Floor,

18th "A" cross, Bhuvaneshwari Nagar,

Hebbal Kempapura,

Bangalore — 560024



  • If and once Your Application is approved ,all details pertaining to your membership will be sent to you either by e-mail or post.
  • The terms and conditions of the membership are mentioned in the application form, please read them carefully.
  • We will not accept Money in the form of CASH or Money Orders , if sent through courier or post.
  • You may also hand over all the relevant documents in a sealed envelope to an authorised BAS representative (Kindly send an e-mail to info@bas.org.in for more details on this matter)
  • For any other queries please contact us at info@bas.org.in
