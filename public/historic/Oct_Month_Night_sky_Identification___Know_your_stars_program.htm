Oct Month Night sky Identification @ Know your stars program


By naveenln; Published 14 Sep 2009

Do you find it difficult to remember the constellations?
Have you ever tried to identify stars in night sky and failed ?
Did you know night sky has a map similar to road maps ?
Can you identify planet Jupiter ? Does your kids / students ask you lot of questions about space ?

Did you know you can learn a lot about the night sky and the star pattern even in the cloudy days ?

Yes, most of your questions are answered in our monthly outreach program "know your stars" at JN Planetarium, Bangalore.

So what are you waiting for.. block your calender for this fantastic program jointly organized by Bangalore Astronomical Society and JN Planetarium every month.

This program is specially designed to all the age groups, who want to "get started" with astronomy. mostly teachers and high school students find it interesting and interactive.

Provided clear skies, we will also arrange for the telescopic observation of moon and Jupiter.

Date : 10 Oct 2009 @ 6:00 PM ( preferred to be at least 15 min before the scheduled time )
Venue : JN Planetarium, High Grounds, Opp Basaveshwara Circle & Chalukya Hotel, Bangalore.

Event Co-ordinator : Naveen Nanjundappa.
