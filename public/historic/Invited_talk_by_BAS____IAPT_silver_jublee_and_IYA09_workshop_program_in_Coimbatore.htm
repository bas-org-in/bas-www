Invited talk by BAS @ IAPT silver jublee and IYA09 workshop program in Coimbatore


By naveenln; Published 15 Jul 2009

Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) Regional Council -13 Tamilnadu and Pondicherry in association with Dept of Physics of Avinashilingam University for Women, Coimbatore TN, has jointly organized one day workshop on "Ancient & Modern Astronomy" as part of IYA 2009 and Silver jubilee celebrations of IAPT.

Bangalore Astronomical Society shall been invited to participate in this workshop and deliver a few talks, and conduct Observation session for participants. Naveen Nanjundappa, Pramod S, Alex are traveling to Coimbatore for conducting this workshop.

Date : 17.July.2009
Time : Full Day Program.
Venue : Avinashilingam University for Women, Coimbatore TN
