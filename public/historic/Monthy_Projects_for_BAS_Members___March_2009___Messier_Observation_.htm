Monthy Projects for BAS Members - March 2009 : Messier Observation.


By naveenln; Published 04 Mar 2009

Dear All,

As part of Monthly Project,

March month will concentrate on following two projects.

1. Messier Object observations : During this month we encourage each member to attempt observation of Messier objects. You can make a note of all the messier objects observed during this month and post it to us. We will update our records. For More information and guidance about messier hunting you can contact our event co-ordinator : Amar sharma ( amar10sharmaa@gmail.com )

2. Audio / Video guide project : BAS will try to create Audio / Video guide for Astronomy covering various topics in Astronomy. BAS Members are encouraged to make full use of this attempt.
we will update you the location where these files will be archived.

If you have an Specific topic for Audio / Video guide project. please send us an email. ( info@bas.org.in or naveen.nanjundappa@gmail.com )

We look forward for all BAS members to actively participate and learn more !
