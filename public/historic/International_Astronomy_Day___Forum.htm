International Astronomy Day @ Forum


By p6; Published 19 Apr 2007

The Bangalore Astronomical Society will conduct a mass-outreach programme on the occasion of The International Astronomy Day at The Forum Mall, Bangalore. Moon and Saturn will be primary observation targets.

You can find details about The Astronomy Day here:
