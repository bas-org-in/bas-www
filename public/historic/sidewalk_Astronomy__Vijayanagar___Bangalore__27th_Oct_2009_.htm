sidewalk Astronomy@ Vijayanagar , Bangalore (27th Oct 2009)


By vivek; Published 25 Oct 2009

As part of the "30 day Side walk Astronomy  programme"
, the Bangalore Astronomical Society is conducting a sky watch session
at Vijayanagar ,Bangalore on the 27th of October 2009 between
6.30 pm and 8.30 pm.The event is free and open to all .We invite you to
join us on this day,  to observe the marvels of the universe the way
Galileo did 400 years ago.

Please contact Amar Sharma from BAS for more details regarding the event.
