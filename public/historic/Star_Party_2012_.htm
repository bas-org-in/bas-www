Star Party 2012.


By admin; Published 16 Jan 2012

We're pleased to announce the first star party of the year 2012. The location will be Agumbe, in the pristine scenic heart of the western ghats.

All else withstanding, we should have a great time this January on the night of 21st.

For full details of the event and registration, please visit - http://crm.bas.org.in/civicrm/event/info?reset=1&id=5

Contact us on info@bas.org.in for queries.
