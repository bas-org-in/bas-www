Observation @ Hosahalli.


By p6; Published 21 Nov 2007

In the wake of crystal clear skies in Bangalore and the great climate in the last few days, BAS is calling for an observation event at Hosahalli village, 70KM off Bangalore on 17th Nov, 2007

Agenda shall be primarily DSO observation, coupled with introductory sessions for novices, Observing Leonid meteos and more. 

 For venue info, please the visit the "Our Venues" pages on the top horizontal bar. For other queries, please use our contact form.
