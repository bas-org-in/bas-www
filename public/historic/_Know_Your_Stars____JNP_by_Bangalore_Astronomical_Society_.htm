"Know Your Stars" @ JNP by Bangalore Astronomical Society.


By naveenln; Published 13 Apr 2009

"Know your stars" is one of the various collaborative activities planned by Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium (JNP) and Bangalore Astronomical Society(BAS)

This Program is held on every second saturday of the month, About 35 people participated. The presentation and the content was organised very well and the program was well appreciate.



Basically covered, "what's up tonight" and a few basic concepts of astronomy.

Later we had a "Ask an Amateur Astronomer" Session, about 25 people actively participated and questions came from almost all participants.

"Are we really seeing stars as they are now ? and are they really there in that position right now ?"
"why we don't see many stars from Bangalore?"
"why does moon change its shape every day?"
and so on..

Then we all moved to the open sky, we had one telescope for observations. Saturn was shown and it was looking good inspite of the cloudy skies.
