How to Connect to IRC without coming here


By admin; Published 01 Oct 2009

a) IRC Clients:

Please google them and download.

Linux: Konversation, Pidgin, et al will do.
Windows: Pidgin, mIRC

OS Agnostic: Chatzilla (Plugin for Firefox)

Web based: Freenode

You will be automatically assigned or prompted to choose a nick.

b) Connection Details

Server: irc.freenode.org
Channel: #b-a-s

Command: (This is a one step copy-paste command on most IRC clients once on
the server. If this doesn't work, go step wise with details mentioned
above):

/join #b-a-s
