The Bangalore Astronomical Society will be conducting a one day workshop on Careers in Planetary Sciences, on the 19th of September 2010 at VET auditorium,VET school,J.P. Nagar -II Phase Bangalore. The Workshop will be delivered by Dr. Vishnu Reddy.

 

About the speaker, Dr. Vishnu Reddy:

Vishnu is probably one of India’s most successful  astronomers.

He has been credited with the discovery of 22 main belt asteroids, two binary near-Earth asteroids, six main-belt binary asteroids, and a supernova by the International Astronomical Union. One of his asteroid discoveries has been named 78118 Bharat in honor of his motherland. Reddy is currently an Assistant Research Professor in the Department of Space Studies at the University of North Dakota. His research involves compositional study of near-Earth asteroids using near-infrared spectroscopy. He is a visiting astronomer at the NASA Infrared Telescope Facility at Mauna Kea, Hawaii and visiting scientist at the prestigious Max-Planck Institute for Solar System Research, Germany, He is a participating investigator on numerous international astronomy research projects and regularly uses observatories around the world.

Who should attend  ?

The Workshop aims at providing an insight into planetary sciences. It is a must attend for all those who are fascinated about planetary Sciences and for those wish to pursue Astronomy as a career. If you feel you want to contribute to Astronomy even as an amateur, this workshop is definitely one to attend.

What can I learn at this workshop?

You can expect to learn a lot of things that will help you in converting your interests in Astronomy to something more meaningful. You can learn to start making scientific contributions as an amateur and learn what it takes to of become a professional astronomer.
