Workshop on Indian Astronomy


By akarshsimha; Published 08 Jun 2009

Dr. S Balachandra Rao, who ranks amongst the experts in the field of Indian Astronomy is conducting a three-day Workshop on "Indian Astronomy - Planetary Positions, Eclipses & Panchanga" scheduled for June 26 to 28, 2009 at Girinagar,Bangalore.

Everyone is invited to participate in the Workshop, which is being held as a part of IYA-2009.

We will be putting up more information here in a while.
