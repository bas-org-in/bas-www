BAS introduces The TELESCOPE LIBRARY


By vivek; Published 07 Jun 2011

The Bangalore Astronomical Society is happy to announce its new facility - The telescope library.

Members of the society can use the facility to borrow telescopes and accessories from BAS for their personal (and non-commercial activity).A nominal maintenance fee will be applicable for each day of usage. The fee will help us to maintain (re-coat mirrors etc. and replace word out parts). We hope that members will use the facility wisely and make the best use of this facility. Application for the Telescope can be made online.

Please Visit the 'TELESCOPE LIBRARY' Tab on the Main menu for the application form.
