Outreach Event at the URise Ashram


By p6; Published 16 Apr 2008

The Bangalore Astronomical Society is organizing an astronomy session for summer camp participants at the URise Ashrama, off Manchinbele, Near Doddaa Aalada Mara, Mysore Road.

The agenda shall involve a basic introduction to astronomy and night observing, coupled up with an interactive session.

Interested parties willing to volunteer for this event are requested to email pavan dot k dot murthy at gmail dot com.
