Side-walk Astronomy @ Jayanagar (south Bangalore)


By vivek; Published 11 Oct 2009

As part of the  "30 day Sidewalk Astronomy"  programme , a sky watch event  will be organised for the general public on the 13th of October 2009 at Jayanagar ,Bangalore.We hope to show the Planet Jupiter and its moons to the people on this day (i.e if the weather permits us to ) .The details of the event are as below:

Venue: 11th Main Park , near Ragavendraswamy mutt , Jayanagar 4th Block
Wikimapia Link: http://wikimapia.org/#lat=12.9192569&lon=77.5853688&z=18&l=0&m=b
