Grand Star Party - BAS and Sheratons Astronomy Club


By vivek; Published 23 Dec 2011

The Bangalore Astronomical Society and Sheratons Astronomy Club are conducting the Grand Star Party at the JGI Global Campus on the 31st of December 2011 and 1st January 2011. The Details of the event are as below:
