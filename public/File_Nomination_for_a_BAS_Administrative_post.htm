File Nomination for a BAS Administrative post


By admin; Published 02 Oct 2012

File your nominations for the BAS 2012 Elections. This form will be open for nominations for a duration of two weeks from the announcement. Election dates will be subsequently planned and announced at convenience of the former council members and the nominees (with a notice in advance for registered members).

Please note that only registered members will be allowed to vote or nominate. If you haven't yet renewed your membership, please do so.
